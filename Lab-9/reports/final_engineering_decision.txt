Additional Questions

• What is transfer learning?
Transfer learning reuses knowledge from a model trained on a large dataset and adapts it to a new task.

• What is a pretrained model?
It is a model already trained before, usually on a big dataset (for example ImageNet).

• What does it mean to freeze layers?
It means keeping selected layers unchanged during training (no weight updates).

• What does it mean to replace the classifier head?
It means replacing the final output layer with a new one matching your target classes.

• What is fine-tuning?
Fine-tuning means unfreezing some pretrained layers and training them on your task data.

• Which approach worked best: SimpleCNN, frozen ResNet18 or fine-tuned ResNet18?
On this run, frozen ResNet18 and fine-tuned ResNet18 tied at 1.0000 accuracy; frozen is better trade-off due lower training time.

• What are the engineering trade-offs between accuracy, training time and model complexity?
SimpleCNN is fastest but less accurate; fine-tuned is most complex and slowest; frozen transfer gives top accuracy with lower complexity than fine-tuning.

• Would you use transfer learning in an operational Earth Observation system? Why?
Yes. It gives strong accuracy on small datasets, faster deployment, and better engineering reliability when combined with OOD monitoring.
