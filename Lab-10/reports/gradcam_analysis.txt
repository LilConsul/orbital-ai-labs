Conclusions:
- Did the model focus on meaningful regions?
Yes, for real images, the model focused on relevant features (texture for forest, riverbank for river, buildings for residential). However, for noise, it focused on random patterns.

- Did suspicious predictions appear?
No suspicious predictions were observed for real images, but the model's behavior on noise indicates it can be misled by irrelevant inputs.

- Can explainability guarantee correctness?
No, explainability can provide insights into the model's decision-making process, but it does not guarantee correctness. A model can focus on seemingly meaningful regions and still make incorrect predictions due to biases in the training data or overfitting.

- Why is XAI important in operational systems?
XAI is crucial in operational systems because it allows stakeholders to understand and trust the model's decisions. It can help identify biases, ensure compliance with regulations, and facilitate debugging and improvement of the model. In critical applications (e.g., healthcare, finance), explainability can be essential for ethical and legal reasons, ensuring that decisions are transparent and justifiable.
