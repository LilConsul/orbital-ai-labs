• Which explanation appears more localized?
The occlusion sensitivity method appears more localized. The visualization is divided into small squares, and each square clearly shows how important that exact region is for the prediction. This makes it easier to identify precise areas that influence the model, compared to Grad-CAM, which tends to blur attention across a larger region.

• Which explanation appears easier to interpret?
Grad-CAM appears easier to interpret. The smooth heatmap and gradual color transitions make it more intuitive to understand where the model is focusing. The output looks more natural and visually aligned with the image, while the blocky structure of occlusion sensitivity requires more effort to interpret

• What happens for unknown inputs?
For unknown inputs, both methods still produce explanations even when the input does not contain meaningful information. As seen in earlier examples, the model can generate attention maps that look structured, even when the input is random noise. This shows that the explanations reflect the model’s internal behavior but do not guarantee that the input itself is meaningful

• Can one explanation method completely replace another?
One explanation method cannot completely replace another. Each method highlights different aspects of the model’s reasoning. Grad-CAM provides a more global and intuitive overview, while occlusion sensitivity offers more precise, localized insights. Relying on only one method can be misleading, since it may hide important details or give a false sense of understanding.
