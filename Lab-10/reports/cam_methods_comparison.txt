• Do all CAM methods highlight the same image regions?
Not exactly. All methods generally focus on the same overall area (right side of the image), which likely contains the important forest-related texture. However, they differ in how much of the region they cover and how concentrated the attention is. Some methods highlight a broader region, while others focus more narrowly.

• Which method produces the sharpest visualization?
EigenCAM produces the sharpest visualization.
It has very clear and concentrated hotspots (bright red areas), making it easy to see exactly where the model is focusing

• Which method produces the smoothest visualization?
Grad-CAM is the smoothest.
Its heatmap transitions gradually from red to blue, without sharp boundaries, giving a more “blurry” but stable view of attention

• Which method appears easiest to interpret?
LayerCAM is probably the easiest to interpret.
It balances both:

clear activation regions (like EigenCAM)
and smooth transitions (like Grad-CAM)

So it gives a good sense of where and how strongly the model is focusing.

• Do any methods produce suspicious or unexpected attention?
Yes, slightly.

Some methods (especially Grad-CAM and HiResCAM) highlight a large portion of the image, including less relevant areas.
This can make it unclear whether the model is truly focusing on meaningful features or just spreading attention widely.

• What does this suggest about relying on only one XAI method?
It suggests that relying on just one method can be misleading.
Different CAM methods:
    highlight different aspects of the model’s reasoning
    may exaggerate or hide certain features
